# Starter prompts

Try these once ZiplineOS is connected. They match the "Try this first" suggestions in ZiplineOS.

## Look around (read-only)

- *List my workflows and summarise the steps in the fact find.*
- *Find every question that captures an address in the home loan workflow.*
- *Which forms have unpublished changes?*
- *Show me the questions on the employment form, in order, with any show/hide rules.*

## Small, safe changes

- *In the employment form, add a required "Trust name" question that only shows when employment
  type is company or trust. Don't publish yet.*
- *Change the self-employed business name question to an ABR lookup and remove the separate ABN
  question. Show me the result before publishing.*
- *Add a minimum of $0 and a maximum of $5,000,000 to the loan amount question.*

## Bigger jobs

- *Build a new "Refinance" workflow: enquiry, fact find, documents, lodged, approved, settled
  (won), not proceeding (lost). Reuse my existing fact-find forms. Show me the plan first.*
- *In the live Home Loan workflow, add a "Valuation" stage after "Lodged". Use a draft and walk
  me through any conflicts.*
